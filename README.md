HR Attrition Dashboard (Power BI)

This project presents an HR Analytics Dashboard built with Power BI to analyze employee attrition patterns. The dashboard helps identify key factors influencing employee turnover such as department, age group, job satisfaction, overtime, and education field.
The goal of this project is to demonstrate data visualization, HR analytics, and business insights generation using Power BI.

📌 Key Metrics
The dashboard highlights important HR indicators:
Total Attrition Count: 237 employees
Attrition Rate: 16.12%
Average Years at Company: 7.01 years
Average Age: 36.92 years
These KPIs provide a quick overview of the company's workforce attrition.

📊 Dashboard Insights
Attrition by Department
Research & Development has the highest employee attrition.
Sales is the second most affected department.
Human Resources shows the lowest attrition.
Attrition by Age Group
Employees aged 30–40 and under 30 show the highest attrition levels.
Employees over 50 have the lowest turnover.
Attrition by Education Field
Life Sciences and Medical fields contribute the most to attrition.
Human Resources and Other fields have lower attrition numbers.
Attrition by Gender
Slightly higher attrition among male employees compared to female employees.
Attrition by Job Satisfaction
Employees with low job satisfaction levels tend to leave more frequently.
Attrition by Overtime
Employees who work overtime show noticeably higher attrition than those who do not.
Attrition by Years at Company
Most employees leave within the first 10 years at the company.
Attrition decreases as tenure increases.
